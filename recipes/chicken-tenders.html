Chicken Tenders

Home

Description

Chicken tenders are a small part located underneath the chicken breasts. This area is also sometimes called chicken tenderloin. In one chicken, there’s usually only 2 piece of chicken tenders. Which is why there is not as many of this part of a chicken like chicken breasts.

A chicken tender tend to have a smaller shape, elongated with a slight taper. The texture tends to be softer than chicken breasts so it is more suitable for dishes that require fast cooking but still require maintaining the soft texture, like deep frying.

Ingredients

For the chicken marinade:

  • 500g chicken tender
  • 3 cloves of garlic, grated finely
  • 3 spoons of full cream milk
  • 1 teaspoon of lemon juice, for the buttermilk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of black pepper
  • 1 1/2 teaspoon of paprika powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon of garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon of chicken essence powder

For the coating flour:

  • 2 cups of wheat flour
  • 1/2 of cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon of paprika powder
  • 1 teaspoon of garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon of onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon of white pepper powder
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 teaspoon of chicken essence powder

For the creamy garlic sauce:

  • 4 to 5 spoon of mayonnaise
  • 1 clove of garlic, finely grated
  • 1 teaspoon of lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoon of honey
  • 1 teaspoon of mustard
  • 1/2 teaspoon of black pepper
  • a pinch of salt
  • a little bit of chopped parsley

Steps

For the chicken marinade:

  1. Make the homemade buttermilk by mixing 3 spoon of full cream milk with 1 teaspoon of lemon juice. Stir and let rest until the milk is slightly thick.
  2. In a big bowl, pour the buttermilk with grated garlic, salt, chicken essence powder, black pepper powder, paprika powder, garlic powder, and egg. Stir until they are blended together.
  3. Mix in the chicken tenders into the marinade until they achieve an even coating
  4. Close the bowl and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes. The longer the it is left to marinate, the juicier and tastier it will be.

For the chicken coating:

  1. Take a piece of marinated chicken tender and mix it in the coating mix. Gently push on the chicken so that coating mix properly sticks on the chicken.
  2. Dip the chicken in cold water for approximately 2 seconds only and immediately lift it.
  3. Mix it again in the coating mix and mix so that an uneven coating is formed. This is to achieve the small crispy pieces after frying.
  4. Tap gently on the chicken to remove excess coating mix, and let rest for 3 to 5 minutes on a separate tray before frying.
  5. Heat the cooking oil at around 175 to 180 degree Celsius, make sure there is enough oil to submerge the chicken pieces.
  6. Fry the chicken pieces either one by one or in small quantities to ensure the oil temperature is constant and to avoid overcrowding.
  7. Fry the chicken until it reaches golden colour and crispness. If extra crispness is desired, fry it a second time after letting it rest for a few minutes.
  8. Toss the freshly fried pieces on top of a rack or oil filter to remove excess oil. Let it rest for 5 minutes so that the coating is stable and maintains its crispness.

For the creamy garlic sauce:

  1. In a small bowl, mix in the mayonnaise, grated garlic, lemon juice, honey, mustard, black pepper powder, and pinch of salt.
  2. Add a little bit of parsley and then mix until the sauce becomes creamy and smooth.
  3. Taste test the sauce. If it needs a little bit of sweetness, add honey. If it needs more freshness, add a little more lemon juice.
  4. Store the sauce in a refrigerator before serving so that texture is thicker
