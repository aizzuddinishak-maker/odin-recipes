Kampung Doughnut

Home

Description

Traditional doughnut that is soft, fluffy and has nice aesthetical white ring on the side.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 teaspoon of dry yeast
  • 1 spoon of sugar
  • 1/2 cup of warm water
  • 3 cups of wheat flour
  • 2 spoon of condensed milk
  • 2 spoon of margarine
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon of salt
  • 1/2 cups of additional water
  • oil for frying
  • Dry granulated sugar for coating the doughnuts

Steps

  1. In a bowl, add warm water, sugar and dry yeast. Stir well and leave for 5 to 10 minutes until foamy. If the yeast is not foamy, do not use it.
  2. In a large bowl, add the wheat flour and salt. Mix well first so that the salt does not come into direct contact with the yeast.
  3. Pour the activated yeast mixture into the flour. Add the beaten eggs, condensed milk and margarine.
  4. Add water little by little while mixing and kneading until you get a soft, elastic and slightly sticky dough. It is not necessary to use up all the water.
  5. Knead the dough for 10 to 15 minutes until it becomes elastic. If you press it with your fingers and the dough springs back, it means the dough is good.
  6. Round the dough and let it rest in a lightly oiled bowl until it has doubled in size. Don't just follow the time, watch the dough expand.
  7. Punch the dough to remove the air. Roll out the dough to about 1.5 cm thick, then with a doughnut mold or by hands, shape the dough into a circle and punch a hole in the middle.
  8. Arrange the doughnuts on non-stick paper. Use up the remaining dough into doughnuts.
  9. Let the doughnuts rest by about 5 to minutes, until they look light and fluffy.
  10. Blend the dry granulated sugar by pulse a few times to make the coating sugar.
  11. Heat up the cooking oil until medium hot. Fry the doughnuts at medium or low heat. Fry until their are light brownish colour then flip on to the other side.
  12. Once both sides of the doughnuts are gold-ish in colour, lift them from the pan and drain excess oil on top of a rack.
  13. Coat the doughnuts while it is still warm so that it sticks.
